What Is An Online UPS?

Your Load Always Runs On Clean Inverter Power

In an online (double-conversion) UPS, incoming mains is converted to DC and back to pure AC continuously. The output never depends on the raw grid, so surges, sags, spikes and outages never reach your equipment.

  • No switching delay when power fails — transfer time is zero
  • Stable voltage and frequency, even on poor or generator power
  • Ideal for servers, CNC and medical equipment, and 24x7 plants

How It Works

Double Conversion, Step By Step

Why the output stays clean whatever happens on the grid.

1

Rectifier

Mains AC is converted to DC and charges the batteries.

2

Battery Bank

Holds energy and takes over instantly if mains fails.

3

Inverter

DC is converted back to fresh, regulated pure AC.

4

Static Bypass

Automatic backup path if the UPS needs service or overloads.
