Two Ways to Add Storage

Off-grid or hybrid — which one do you need?

A pure off-grid system ignores the grid entirely. Solar charges a battery bank, and your loads run from those batteries. It is the right choice for farms, tube wells, telecom sites, border locations and anywhere the grid is absent or hopeless.

A hybrid system does both. It offsets your daily consumption like an on-grid plant, keeps a battery reserve for outages, and can still draw from the grid when solar and battery are both short. For most homes and shops with frequent cuts, hybrid is the sensible middle ground.

  • Essential loads stay live through long outages
  • Lithium banks for compact size and long cycle life
  • Tubular batteries where budget matters more than footprint
  • Battery bank sized against your genuine backup hours, not a round number
  • Integrates with existing inverter and UPS installations

Storage Options

Choosing Your Battery Bank

Battery choice drives cost, footprint and how much daily maintenance the system needs.

Compact & Long-Life

Lithium Battery Bank

  • 48V configurations including 100 Ah banks
  • Much longer cycle life than lead-acid
  • No topping up, no acid fumes, no ventilation room
  • Deeper usable discharge from the same rating
  • Small footprint — wall or rack mounted

Budget Friendly

Tubular Battery Bank

  • Proven technology, widely serviceable locally
  • Lower upfront cost per kWh of storage
  • Needs a ventilated space and periodic water topping
  • Shorter life than lithium in deep-cycle use
  • Suits backup-only duty with modest daily cycling

Sealed & Maintenance Free

SMF / VRLA Bank

  • Maintenance-free sealed construction
  • Common in UPS and telecom rooms
  • No water topping required
  • Good where access for servicing is limited
  • Amaron Quanta and equivalent options available
